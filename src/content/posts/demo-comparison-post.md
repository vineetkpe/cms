Comparison articles work best when the reader can understand the decision quickly and then inspect the evidence.

## Quick verdict

Open with a short summary explaining which option suits which type of reader. Avoid declaring a universal winner when the trade-offs differ by use case.

## What we compared

| Criteria | Option A | Option B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Beginners | Advanced users |
| Setup | Simple | Flexible |
| Learning curve | Lower | Higher |

## Option A

Explain its strongest use cases, important limitations, and who should choose it.

### Pros

- Easy to start
- Clear workflow
- Lower complexity

### Cons

- Less customization
- May be limiting for advanced workflows

## Option B

Explain where the second option is stronger and where the additional complexity matters.

## Final recommendation

End with a decision rule rather than repeating the whole article. The reader should know which option fits their situation.
