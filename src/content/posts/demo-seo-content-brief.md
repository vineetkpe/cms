A useful content brief should make the writer's job easier, not bury them under keyword lists.

## Start with the reader's problem

Write one sentence describing the exact problem the article should solve. This keeps the article focused and reduces unnecessary sections.

## Define search intent

Clarify whether the reader wants to learn, compare, troubleshoot, buy, or complete a task. The article format should match that intent.

## Build the outline around questions

Use a logical heading structure. Each major section should answer a real question or complete one part of the reader's task.

### Add supporting evidence

Plan where examples, screenshots, original observations, data, or expert sources are needed. This is more useful than repeating the target keyword.

## Plan internal links

Add relevant links to supporting articles and important pages. Internal links help readers discover related material and help search engines understand site structure.

## Final editorial checklist

- Clear title and description
- One primary topic
- Useful subheadings
- Accurate claims
- Descriptive image alt text
- Relevant internal links
- Strong conclusion or next step
